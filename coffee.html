FAQ

Capsule Coffee Maker questions.

Does it need a power outlet?
No. It is rated ≤36 V and runs cordless, so it brews from a charged body with no cord to plug in while it pours.
Can it make both hot and cold coffee?
Yes — hot extraction and cold extraction both come from the same unit, chosen with the one touch button on the body.
Do I have to use capsules?
No. The kit includes a 5.1 cm powder bowl, so you can brew your own ground coffee as well as capsules.
How much coffee does one fill make?
The tank is listed at 0.5 litres and below, rated for 6–10 cups.
What exactly is in the kit?
The extractor, the bracket stand and the 5.1 cm powder bowl — the three pieces you need to brew. It is one size, five colourways, and the same stand fits all five.
How do I brew with it?
Set the bracket stand on a flat surface, load a capsule or the powder bowl, pick hot or cold with the touch button, then press and let the pump pour into your cup.
Is there a warranty?
Yes — 12 months, as stated on the supplier listing for this SKU (model ZM0022).
