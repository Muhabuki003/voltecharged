How long does the battery last?

Yes. The 200W motor is built to crush ice, frozen fruit and greens, and the blend takes about ten seconds.

Both are single-serve. 380ml is the compact everyday cup; 420ml gives you a little more room for a full smoothie with ice. The size you pick here is the size that goes in your bag.
