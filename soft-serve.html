FAQ

Soft Serve Maker questions.

Do I need ice or rock salt?
No. The inner bowl carries its own coolant, so it freezes the mix itself — no ice and no rock salt. You freeze the bowl ahead, add your mix, and press the button.
How long does a batch take?
About 15 minutes of gentle churning, at roughly 34 rpm.
How much does one batch make?
500ml — about a pint — per batch. The bowl is designed to make one generous bowl or two smaller servings.
What can I make in it?
Soft serve, sorbet and frozen yoghurt. Chilled cream, yoghurt, fruit purée or a sorbet base all work as the mix.
How big is it, and how much power?
It is a compact countertop unit: 12W, 14.3 × 14.3 × 16.5 cm, about 1.1 kg. It takes a US plug.
Which colours are there?
Four: Peach, Light Blue, Pink and Sky Blue. All four are $49.99 and all four ship free.
Shipping and returns?
Free shipping and a 30-day money-back guarantee. Ships from Texas.
